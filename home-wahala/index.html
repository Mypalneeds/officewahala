Home Wahala: the African mum game

Mummy don vex. Run your errands, dodge the slipper, greet your elders, and whatever you do, don't take Aunty's money while she is watching.

In short: Home Wahala is the free childhood-nostalgia half of Office Wahala. You spend a day at home with a Nigerian mum who sends you on errands, chases you with her slipper and the broom, counts to three and reports everything to Daddy. Office Wahala closes at weekends, so on Saturdays and Sundays you play at home. No sign-up, no download.

Mummy's moves

  • The chase: Mummy comes in from the side or the top and follows you for a few seconds. Outrun her for +120. If she catches you, you lose two lives and hear "Will you do it again?!"
  • The slipper: while she chases you she throws it. It curves towards you, so move late and move sideways.
  • "I will count to three": she stands still and counts. Get to her before three for +200. Miss it and she comes for you, faster.
  • The broom and the wooden spoon: the broom sweeps in from the side after a warning; spoons, homework and footballs fall from above.
  • Mummy's mood: a meter at the top of the screen. It rises when you skip greetings, fail errands, take two pieces of meat or get reported. When it fills up, she comes after you vexed.

Errands

  • Fetch water: grab the keg, then carry it home for a few seconds. You move slower, and one hit spills it.
  • Buy bread and Maggi: grab both, then bring back the change. Each hit costs ₦50 of it. Bring back all ₦200 for the Correct change badge.
  • Go and return it: "I said Titus, not Kote!" Grab the right fish and leave the wrong one.
  • Wash the plates: grab five plates before Mummy wakes up. She won't chase you while she naps, but if time runs out she comes.

Home training

  • Greet your elders: visiting aunties wait at the side. Stand next to one for a moment to greet her (+150). Walk past without greeting and Mummy hears about it.
  • Aunty's money: after you greet her, she may hold out ₦500. A small window shows Mummy's face. Take it while she is watching and she gets angry; take it when she looks away and it goes into your pocket money.
  • One meat: at parties and at Christmas, the first piece of meat is fine. The second one, while Mummy is watching? "Ehn?! Who sent you?"

The rest of the family

  • Big sister follows you around. If she touches you, she tells Mummy, and Mummy comes straight away.
  • Mama Ngozi from next door stops you for gist (you slow down) and reports you.
  • Daddy only turns up late in the day. He is slow, but if he catches you: "Come to my room." The day is over.
  • Grandma is a power-up: while she's around, nobody can touch you, not even Daddy.

Power-ups

Grandma (shield), cold Malt from the freezer (speed, but Mummy will ask who drank it), Daddy home early (Mummy is busy with him for a while), a report card with 1st position (Mummy calms down completely) and "under the bed" (keep it for later and hide when she comes).

Home events

Sunday service (keep moving or you'll fall asleep in church), a party in the compound (jollof everywhere, one meat only), Christmas rice in December, the lesson teacher, "Off that TV!" (only grab the remote when Mummy looks away) and a stream of visitors to greet. NEPA still takes light.

Houses

Start in a face-me-I-face-you yard in Mushin, then unlock a family house in Ibadan, a family compound in Enugu and an estate duplex in Gwarinpa with home points. The village opens every December.

Why the office closes at weekends

Because nobody should be at work on Saturday. Office Wahala, its daily shift, career mode and league shifts are closed on Saturdays and Sundays (Lagos time). Leaderboards, the shop and your desk stay open, and Home Wahala is there every day of the week.

Questions

What is Home Wahala?

Home Wahala is the second game inside Office Wahala. Instead of the office, you spend a day at home with Mummy: you run errands, dodge her slipper, the broom and the wooden spoon, greet visiting aunties and keep Mummy's mood from boiling over. It is free, needs no sign-up and plays on phones and computers.

When can I play Home Wahala?

Every day. Office Wahala is closed on Saturdays and Sundays (Lagos time), so at weekends Home Wahala is the only game open. On weekdays you can switch between the office and home on the menu.

What happens if Mummy catches me?

You lose two lives and get a short lecture: "Will you do it again?!" Nobody is ever shown being hit. Stars spin round your head, Mummy calms down a little and the day goes on.

What does "I will count to three" do?

Mummy stands still and counts. Reach her before she says three and you get points and she calms down. If you don't, she comes after you, faster than usual, throwing her slipper.

Can I take the money visitors give me?

Only when Mummy is not looking. A small window shows whether she is watching. Take the aunty's money while she is watching and Mummy gets angry; take it when she looks away and it goes into your pocket money.

Is Home Wahala free?

Yes. Like Office Wahala it is completely free, with no sign-up, no download and no pop-up ads. The naira in the game is pretend money and can never be bought or cashed out.
