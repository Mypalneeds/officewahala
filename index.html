How to play

On a computer

←↑↓→ or WASD to move

On a phone

Put one finger anywhere on the screen and drag. Your worker follows your finger.

Grab these

  • Salary +100
  • Data bundle +75
  • Lunch money +50
  • Leave approval: nothing can touch you
  • Generator fuel: speed boost, and light during blackouts
  • Friday bonus +500, rare, and a life back
  • Keep-for-later power-ups: auto-reply, fake call, puff-puff. Tap Use or press E

Dodge these

  • Oga. Costs two lives. Outrun him for +100
  • "Quick" meetings

  • Last-minute deadlines

  • Runaway printers
  • The colleague with "one small task"
  • MD's driver: dashes across, watch the warning
  • HR lady: her query letters follow you
  • Aunty from Accounts: freezes your salary pickups
