Privacy

Office Wahala collects as little as possible. Here is exactly what is stored and why.

Effective 2026-10-27

Summary: No cookies, no ad networks, no tracking across other websites. The game counts anonymous gameplay statistics to improve itself, and you can switch them off in Settings. Your progress lives on your own device.

Saved on your device

Your best scores, wallet, upgrades, outfits, badges, settings, name and your Oga's name are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us (except your name and weekly best when you join an office league, see below). Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To understand how the game is played and fix problems, the game sends anonymous events to our own server on Netlify, for example: a page was opened, a shift started or ended, its score and length, which boss or event appeared, which feature was used, and error messages. Each event comes with:

  • A random ID created on your device (not linked to your name, phone number or any account)
  • The website that sent you here, if any, and campaign tags in the link
  • Device type (phone, tablet or computer), screen size range, language and the country your connection comes from

We do not store IP addresses, names, phone numbers or exact locations in the statistics, and we don't use cookies. Statistics are used only to improve the game and are never sold. Turn them off any time in Settings → Anonymous stats. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, statistics start switched off.

Sponsor billboards

Some billboards on the road, screens on the office walls and a small card on the menu show sponsors. They are drawn by the game itself; no ad network is involved and advertisers receive no information about you. The game counts how often each sponsor is seen or tapped, anonymously, in the statistics above. Tapping a sponsor opens their website, where their own privacy policy applies.

Office leagues and challenges

When you play for an office league, the name you chose, your scores, time, level, number of tries and any job title or gold name you bought are stored on that league's board for the round, linked to your random device ID. Anyone with the league code can see the board. A league creator's admin PIN is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value, and the board never shows device IDs. In leagues with the same office for everyone, your best run is also stored as a list of positions so others can race your ghost. Office wall posts (ready-made lines only), reactions and lunch-money gifts are stored with your chosen name; reactions and gifts use a scrambled version of your device ID. Challenge links contain your name and score in the link itself, so whoever you share them with can see them.

Reviews

If you send a review, the message, star rating and optional name are delivered to the developer through Netlify Forms. If you use the WhatsApp option instead, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

Services we use

The game is hosted on Netlify, which processes standard server logs to keep the service running. If voices are on, your device's built-in speech engine may be used to speak lines.
