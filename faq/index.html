Office Wahala FAQ

Straight answers about Office Wahala, the free Nigerian office arcade game.

In short: Office Wahala is a free browser game made in Lagos. You dodge your boss (Oga), meetings and deadlines, collect your salary and survive the workday. It needs no download or sign-up, works on phones and offline, and comes in English and Pidgin.

Is Office Wahala free?

Yes. Office Wahala is completely free to play. There are no pop-up or video ads, no in-app purchases with real money and no sign-up. A few sponsor billboards appear on the road and office walls. The naira in the game's wallet is pretend money you earn by playing.

Do I need to download or sign up?

No. It runs in any modern web browser at officewahala.netlify.app. You can optionally add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Does Office Wahala work on phones?

Yes. It works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and computers. On a phone you drag one finger anywhere on the screen and your worker follows.

Can I play Office Wahala offline?

Yes, after your first visit. The game saves itself on your device, so it opens and plays without data. Install it to your home screen for the easiest access: on Android use the Install app button in the menu; on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How do I beat Oga in Office Wahala?

When the red warning appears, move away from the side Oga is coming from, then keep moving in wide circles. If he doesn't catch you before he gives up, you earn +100 points. A Fake call power-up sends him away instantly, and Leave approval makes you untouchable.

What does 'NEPA took light' mean in the game?

It's a power cut. From level 3 the lights go out for about seven seconds and you can only see around your worker. The phrase comes from NEPA, Nigeria's old power authority; Nigerians still say 'NEPA took light' when electricity goes off and shout 'Up NEPA!' when it returns.

How is the score calculated?

You earn points every second you survive, multiplied by your level. Pickups add more: salary +100, data bundle +75, lunch +50, Friday bonus +500. Reports at glowing desks give +300, escaping Oga +100, surviving a blackout +150 and arriving early +100. Streaks of pickups without a hit multiply your points up to three times.

What is the boss key?

Press B on a computer (or use the button on the pause screen) and the game instantly turns into a fake spreadsheet called Q3_Revenue_Report_FINAL_v7.xlsx, tab title included. Press B or Esc to come back.

Will the game make noise at work?

Not unless you want it to. Sound effects, music and voices are all off until you turn them on with the speaker button or in Settings.

How do challenge links work?

After a shift, tap Challenge a colleague. It shares your score with a link. Anyone who opens the link sees your score on the menu and a 'Beat' target at the top of the screen while they play.

What are office leagues?

Private scoreboards for a team or office. One person creates a league, sets the rules and shares its 6-letter code or invite link. Players press Play on the league to play a shift that counts for it. The creator chooses a weekly league (resets every Monday, Lagos time) or a tournament with a start and end time, plus the shift length, number of tries, best score or total score, the same office for everyone, whether shop upgrades and the commute are allowed, and an optional prize. They manage it with an admin PIN.

Is there a Pidgin version?

Yes. Switch Language to Pidgin in Settings and all the menus, in-game messages and voice lines change to Nigerian Pidgin.

Where is my best score saved?

On your own device, in your browser's storage. Clearing your browser's site data, or playing in a private window, resets it. Scores you play for an office league are also kept on that league's board for the round.

What data does Office Wahala collect?

Only anonymous gameplay statistics, such as how long shifts last and which features get used, to improve the game. There are no cookies and no advertising trackers, and you can switch the statistics off in Settings. See the privacy page for full details.

Is Office Wahala suitable for children?

The game has no ads, no chat and no strangers contacting players. Its humour is mild and about office life, so it suits teenagers and adults; younger children may not get the workplace jokes.

Can my company use it for team fun?

Yes. Create an office league for your team and play the daily shift each day so everyone gets the same office. There's nothing to install or pay for.

What happens when I unlock all the badges?

Badges never run out. Each of the 30 badges levels up from Bronze to Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond, then Diamond II, III and beyond, with a bigger goal and a naira reward at every level. Getting all 19 classic badges to Gold adds a gold frame to your ID photo and pays ₦5,000. There are also three new weekly challenges every Monday.

Can I send money to another player?

You can send a colleague in one of your office leagues lunch money: ₦100, ₦200 or ₦500 from your in-game wallet, up to ₦500 a day. It's pretend money for the game only. It can't be bought with real money or cashed out.

Can I chat with other players?

Each office league has a wall where you can post ready-made Nigerian office lines like "Who send you?" or "Rematch tomorrow!" and react to posts. There is no free typing, which keeps it friendly. In same-office leagues you also race a ghost of the leader's best run.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Use the Drop a review box on the game's menu, or send a WhatsApp message to 07066459689.
