Questions

How do I win the Office Wahala Weekly Cup?

Open the game, tap the Weekly Cup card on the menu and play. Your best single run counts. The players with the highest scores when the cup ends win the prizes shown on the cup. It is free to enter.

How are Weekly Cup winners paid?

When the cup ends, winners see a private claim code in the game and tap Claim on WhatsApp. The organiser checks the winning scores and sends the prize by bank transfer, airtime, gift card or voucher, then marks it paid on this page.

Do I need to sign up to win?

No. There are no accounts. Your device keeps a private ID and only the winning device can see its claim code. You only share payment details with the organiser on WhatsApp if you win.

Is there an Office Cup and a Home Cup?

Yes. The Office Cup runs Monday to Friday because the office is closed at weekends. The Home Cup is played in Home Wahala and is open every day.
