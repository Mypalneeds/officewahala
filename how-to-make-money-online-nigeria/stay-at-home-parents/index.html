How to make money online from home as a stay-at-home parent

Your hours are broken into small pieces, so your work needs to be too. These options fit around school runs and naps.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Good options for stay-at-home parents are a home business sold online (food, baking, small chops, fashion, hair), virtual assistance in fixed hours, online tutoring after school runs, and reselling through WhatsApp. Pick work you can pause and restart, and be wary of "work from home" offers that ask for a starter fee.

Key facts

  • Asynchronous work (no live meetings) fits around children best.
  • WhatsApp Business catalogues let you sell without a website.
  • Many "work from home" scams ask for a registration fee.
  • Set two protected work blocks a day rather than working all day.

Ideas that fit family life

  • Food business: take orders on WhatsApp for weekend deliveries.
  • Virtual assistant: manage inboxes, bookings and social media for small businesses.
  • Tutoring: evening lessons for primary and secondary pupils.
  • Reselling: children's clothes, school supplies, household items.
  • Content: parenting, recipes or home tips, if you enjoy being on camera.

A routine that works

Choose two blocks a day, for example during the school run window and after bedtime. Batch tasks: reply to all orders at once, cook in bulk, schedule posts in one sitting.

Build trust when customers cannot see you

Online buyers worry about paying someone they have never met. Post real photos of your work, share delivery videos, ask happy customers for short voice notes or screenshots you can repost, and keep a clear refund rule. For services, offer a small first job at a fair price so new clients can test you without much risk.

Launch from home

  1. Pick what you already do well. Cooking, baking, sewing, hair, teaching or organising: start with what friends already praise.
  2. Test with ten customers. Sell to people you know first and ask for honest feedback.
  3. Set order days. Take orders until a cut-off day so you can plan around the children.
  4. Open a WhatsApp Business catalogue. Add prices, photos and your delivery areas.

Questions people ask

What can I sell from home in Nigeria?

Food, baked goods, clothing, hair and beauty services, crafts, and digital services like design or typing.

Do I need a website?

No. A WhatsApp Business catalogue and an Instagram page are enough to start.

Are "data entry from home" jobs real?

Some are. Avoid any that charge you to start or for "training materials".
