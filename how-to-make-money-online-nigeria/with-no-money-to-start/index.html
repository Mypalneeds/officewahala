How to make money online in Nigeria with no money to start

When you have no capital, your capital is your time, your skills and the people you know. That is enough to start.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: With no money, start by selling a service, not a product: tutoring, typing, design on a phone, social media help for small businesses, errands or cleaning booked online. Pre-orders let you sell products without stock. Most importantly, avoid any offer that asks for a "small" fee to start earning: when money is tight, those losses hurt most.

Key facts

  • Services need skill and time, not capital.
  • Pre-orders (customers pay before you buy) remove stock risk.
  • Free learning resources are enough for most beginner skills.
  • Free contests, like the Office Wahala Weekly Cup, never cost you anything.

Zero-capital ideas

  • Services: tutoring, typing, CV writing, phone-based design, social media management.
  • Pre-order selling: collect orders and payment, then buy and deliver.
  • Affiliate links: recommend products you genuinely use to people who trust you.
  • Referral programmes: legitimate apps sometimes pay for genuine sign-ups, without you paying anything.

The "small fee" trap

"Just ₦2,000 to activate your account" sounds small. It rarely stays small. Platforms that charge to start usually earn from new members, not from real work. See games and platforms that pay without a deposit.

Turn the first ₦10,000 into tools

Your first earnings are your seed money. Before spending them, ask what would let you earn more next month: more data for client calls, a ring light for product photos, a paid template pack, or a short course in a skill clients ask for. Spending the first few payments on tools is how a zero-capital hustle becomes a small business.

Start with ₦0

  1. Write down three things people ask you for. Help with homework, phones, cooking, writing, hair: these are your services.
  2. Choose one and set a price. Check what others charge nearby and online.
  3. Tell your network. Post on your WhatsApp status every day for a week with examples.
  4. Use pre-orders for products. Collect payment first, then buy and deliver.

Questions people ask

Can I really start an online business with ₦0?

Yes, if it is a service or pre-order business. You need a phone, some data and a skill.

What skill can I learn for free?

Writing, basic design, spreadsheets, social media management and video editing all have good free tutorials.

Should I borrow money to invest in an online opportunity?

Be very careful. Borrowing to invest in schemes promising returns is how many people lost money in collapses like CBEX.
