Online side hustles for Nigerian office workers

Salary week comes once a month. These side hustles can add something in between, after the 5pm meeting finally ends.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: The best side hustles for office workers reuse the skills you use at work: Excel and reporting, writing, design, HR documents, accounting or IT support, sold as freelance or consulting work in the evenings and weekends. Check your employment contract for conflict-of-interest rules, never use company time or data, and start with one or two clients.

Key facts

  • Read your contract: some employers restrict outside work.
  • Never use company laptops, data or clients for your side hustle.
  • Evenings and weekends suit async freelance work.
  • Many office skills (spreadsheets, writing, HR, accounting) sell well to small businesses.

Office skills that sell

  • Spreadsheets and reports: build templates and dashboards for SMEs.
  • HR documents: handbooks, contracts and job descriptions for small companies.
  • Bookkeeping: monthly records for shops and startups.
  • Writing and editing: proposals, CVs and company profiles.
  • IT support: set up email, devices and websites for small offices.

Keep it clean with your employer

Do your side work outside office hours, on your own devices, for clients who are not your employer's customers or competitors. If in doubt, ask HR before HR sends you a query.

Package your skill as a product

Small businesses buy outcomes, not hours. Instead of "I can do Excel", offer "a monthly sales report template set up in one week" or "a staff handbook for companies under 20 people". A clear package with a fixed price is easier to sell, easier to deliver in the evenings, and easier for happy clients to recommend.

Start around your 9-to-5

  1. Check your contract. Look for clauses on outside work, competition and confidentiality.
  2. Choose one package. Turn a work skill into a fixed-price service for small businesses.
  3. Find the first client in your network. Friends who run businesses are the quickest first customers.
  4. Protect your rest. Cap the hours, and keep at least one day a week fully off.

Questions people ask

Is it legal to have a side hustle in Nigeria?

Generally yes, but your employment contract may restrict outside work or competing business. Check it, and ask if unsure.

How many hours a week should a side hustle take?

Start with five to ten hours. Grow only if your main job and rest do not suffer.
