How to make money online during NYSC

The service year gives you a monthly allowance, free afternoons and a new city. It is a good year to start an online income.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Corps members do best with freelancing in a skill from their course or SAED training, online tutoring, remote internships that turn into jobs, and selling services in their new community. Use the year to build a portfolio and references; avoid "corper investment" schemes that promise to multiply your allowance.

Key facts

  • NYSC's SAED programme teaches skills you can sell online.
  • Remote internships often lead to post-service jobs.
  • Corps members are frequent targets of "allowance doubling" schemes.
  • Keep your PPA duties first; online work fits around them.

What works for corpers

  • Freelance your degree: accountants do bookkeeping, engineers do drawings, English graduates write and edit.
  • Teach online: many corpers already teach at their PPA; offer evening lessons to more students online.
  • Remote internship: a few months of remote work gives you a reference and often an offer.
  • Local services online: design, photography or catering for your new community, promoted on WhatsApp.

Protect your allowance

Groups promising to double your allowance by "trading" or "investing" are a common trap. The SEC has warned about unregistered schemes on WhatsApp and Telegram. See the 9 red flags.

Leave service year with a portfolio

Whatever you earn, the bigger prize is proof. Keep a simple folder of everything you do: before-and-after designs, reports you wrote, students you taught and their results, and short reviews from clients and your PPA supervisor. When service ends, that folder is what gets you the next job or client, especially for remote roles where employers cannot meet you in person.

A plan for the service year

  1. Month 1: settle in. Learn your PPA routine and find out which afternoons and weekends are free.
  2. Month 2: pick your skill. Choose one from your degree or SAED and make three samples.
  3. Months 3 to 6: first clients. Offer your service locally and online. Aim for steady reviews, not big money.
  4. Months 7 to 11: grow and apply. Raise prices, apply for remote internships and jobs with your portfolio.

Questions people ask

Can corps members legally do freelance work?

Yes, as long as it does not interfere with your PPA and NYSC duties.

What skill should a corper learn for online work?

One linked to your degree or SAED training, so you start with an advantage.
