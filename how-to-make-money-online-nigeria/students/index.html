How to make money online as a student in Nigeria

You have more time than money and more skill than you think. Here is how to turn that into income without failing your courses.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: The best options for Nigerian students are tutoring (WAEC, JAMB and course subjects), campus services sold online (typing, project formatting, design, printing runs), freelancing in a skill you are learning, and part-time remote work. Start small, keep it to a few hours a week during exams, and never pay to "join" an earning scheme.

Key facts

  • Your classmates and juniors are your first customers.
  • A WhatsApp status can be your first shop.
  • Under-18s need a parent or guardian for most payment accounts.

Ideas that fit a student timetable

  • Tutoring: JAMB and WAEC prep for secondary school students, online or in small groups.
  • Typing and formatting: projects, CVs and seminar slides for other students.
  • Design: event flyers for departments, fellowships and campus businesses.
  • Content: short videos explaining hard topics in your course.
  • Reselling: buy in bulk, sell to hostel mates through WhatsApp.

Protect your grades

Set a weekly limit, say ten hours, and pause client work two weeks before exams. A first-class or a strong second-class upper will pay you more over a lifetime than any side hustle.

How to price as a student

Students often charge too little and then resent the work. Ask three people already doing the same service what they charge, start a little below them, and raise your price after every five happy customers. Take part payment upfront for big jobs like project formatting, and always agree the deadline in writing on WhatsApp so there is no argument later.

Scams aimed at students

Watch for "earn while you study" apps that ask for a registration fee, fake scholarship and grant offers that need a "processing fee", and loan apps that harass your contacts. A real opportunity does not charge you to apply.

Start in two weeks

  1. List what you are good at. Your strongest subjects, software you know, things classmates ask you for help with.
  2. Choose one service. Pick the one people already ask you for. That is your proof of demand.
  3. Set a price and a WhatsApp catalogue. Add the service, price and three examples to a WhatsApp Business catalogue.
  4. Tell 20 people. Post on your status, class groups and department pages. Ask satisfied customers for a screenshot review.

Questions people ask

Can a student make money online without a laptop?

Yes. Tutoring, selling, design apps and short-video editing can all be done on a phone.

Are "student ambassador" earning schemes legit?

Some are real marketing jobs. If you must pay to become an ambassador, it is not a job.
