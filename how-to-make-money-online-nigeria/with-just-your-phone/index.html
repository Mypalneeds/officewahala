How to make money online in Nigeria with just your phone

No laptop? Many Nigerian online earners started with only a phone. Here is what you can do with one.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: With only a phone you can sell on WhatsApp and Instagram, design graphics and edit short videos with mobile apps, tutor over voice and video calls, do customer support and social media management for small businesses, and create content. A laptop helps later for freelancing at scale, but it is not needed to start.

Key facts

  • WhatsApp Business is free and has a product catalogue.
  • Mobile design and video apps are enough for social media work.
  • Keep a separate number or profile for business if you can.
  • Office Wahala runs in your phone's browser and works offline.

What you can do on a phone

  • Social selling: a WhatsApp Business catalogue and status updates.
  • Design: flyers and posts for churches, events and shops.
  • Video editing: short clips for brands and creators.
  • Tutoring: voice-note and video-call lessons.
  • Social media management: post and reply for small businesses.

Phone habits that help

  • Back up your work to the cloud so a lost phone is not a lost business.
  • Use a password manager and two-step verification on WhatsApp.
  • Buy data in bundles and edit offline when you can.

When a phone is not enough

Some work is hard on a small screen: long documents, large spreadsheets, coding and full-length video editing. If clients keep asking for those, that is the moment to save for a laptop, or to rent time at a hub or cyber café for the heavy jobs while your phone handles messages, design and selling.

Set up your phone business

  1. Install WhatsApp Business. Add a profile photo, description, hours and a catalogue.
  2. Install one design or editing app. Learn it with free tutorials until you can make a flyer in 15 minutes.
  3. Make five samples. Post them on your status and Instagram.
  4. Turn on security. Enable two-step verification and back up your chats and files.

Questions people ask

Can I do freelancing on my phone?

You can start, especially for design, editing, writing and support. Some platforms and clients will expect a laptop later.

Which phone do I need?

Any recent Android or iPhone with a good camera and enough storage for your apps works.
