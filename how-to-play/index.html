How to play Office Wahala

Everything you need to survive a shift at Wahala & Sons Ltd: controls, scoring, every power-up, every boss and the best tips.

Updated 2026-10-27 · About 6 minutes to read

Quick answer: In Office Wahala you move a Nigerian office worker up an endless corridor. Use the ←↑↓→ arrow keys or WASD on a computer, or drag one finger anywhere on a phone. Dodge falling files, "quick" meetings, deadlines and your boss (Oga), grab salary, lunch and data bundles, and survive as long as you can. You have three lives, and every 20 seconds is one office hour from 8:00am.

How scoring works

Your score grows every second you survive, multiplied by your level (so later hours are worth more). On top of that:

What you doPoints
Salary (₦ note)+100
Data bundle+75
Lunch money (jollof pack)+50, more with the canteen card upgrade
Friday bonus (TGIF star)+500 and one life back
Submit a report at a glowing desk+300
Outrun Oga until he gives up+100
Get through a NEPA blackout without a hit+150
Arrive at work before 8:00am+100
Brush past an obstacle without touching it ("close call")+10
Every 5 pickups in a row without a hit (a streak)bonus points, and your multiplier rises to ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5 and ×3

Getting hit costs one life and resets your streak. Oga costs two lives. Scores never go below zero.

The morning commute

Every shift starts on a Lagos road at 7:45am. For 15 seconds, dodge okadas (motorbike taxis), kekes (tricycles), danfo buses, potholes and agberos asking "Owo da?". Each hit makes you 10 minutes late but doesn't cost a life. Arrive late and Oga sees you: you start the office part with one fewer life. Arrive on time for +100. After your first commute a Skip commute button appears, and you can switch the commute off in Settings.

Keep-for-later power-ups

From level 2 you can pick up power-ups that wait in your pocket until you choose to use them (one at a time):

  • Auto-reply: "Out of office". Clears every obstacle on the screen.
  • Fake call: "Sir, MD is calling you". Sends every boss away and keeps them away for a while.
  • Puff-puff: treats for the team. Colleagues, Aunty from Accounts and HR stop chasing you for 8 seconds.

NEPA blackouts

From level 3 the power goes out now and then for about seven seconds. You can only see a circle around your worker (bigger if you have generator fuel), plus glowing pickups. Survive the blackout without a hit for +150. The stabilizer upgrade makes blackouts shorter.

Game modes

Normal shift

Play as long as you can survive. Your personal best is saved on your device.

Daily shift

Everyone gets the same obstacle pattern on the same day, so colleagues compete fairly. Shop upgrades are switched off.

Career mode

A five-day work week with one goal a day. Finish the goal to clock out early and earn a bonus; finish Friday to get a shareable promotion letter. Goals get harder every week.

Challenges and office leagues

After a shift, tap Challenge a colleague to share a link with your score. Whoever opens it sees "Beat Tunde: 2,450" while they play. Office leagues are private scoreboards. Open Office leagues on the menu to see the leagues you're in, then press Play on one: that shift counts for the league, under its rules. The creator picks a weekly league or a tournament with set times, the shift length, how many tries each person gets, best score or total score, the same office for everyone, and whether upgrades and the commute are allowed. They can add a prize, edit the rules, remove players and start new rounds with their admin PIN. Past winners are kept on the board.

In leagues with the same office for everyone, you race a see-through ghost of the leader's best run. Each league has an office wall for ready-made lines and reactions, and you can send a colleague lunch money (₦100–₦500 a day, pretend money only) from the league board.

Offices, missions and the 5pm meeting

You start at a Lagos ad agency. Career promotions (or enough total points) open new offices, each with its own boss and hazard:

  • Bank HQ, Marina: ATM queues block part of the hall; the Branch Manager wants targets.
  • Federal ministry, Abuja: protocol convoys race across the corridor; the Hon. Director wants your memo minuted.
  • Oil and gas company, Port Harcourt: hard hats fall from racks and gas-leak gusts push you sideways; watch for the warning.
  • Tech startup, Yaba: surprise stand-up meetings appear in your path; the Founder wants it shipped today.

During a shift, short missions pop up in the HUD (grab 3 lunch packs, don't get hit for 12 seconds, get 2 close calls). Finish them for points and naira. When the office clock reaches 5pm, the boss calls an emergency meeting and throws agenda papers for 20 seconds; survive it for +1,000 points and ₦500. Daily shifts and league shifts always use the Lagos agency so everyone plays the same office.

Register, seasons, stickers and State Wars

Sign the attendance register once a day for ₦100 to ₦1,000 (day 7 adds a sticker). One missed day is forgiven. Each month is a season with 12 free reward tiers. Sticker envelopes sometimes fall during shifts; collect sets of six for ₦3,000, and doubles turn into ₦50. Pick your state to add your scores to State Wars every week, decorate your desk from the shop, and share your invite link: you and your friend each get ₦1,000 when they finish their first shift.

Home Wahala (and weekends)

Office Wahala is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. Use the Office | Home switch on the menu to play Home Wahala instead, any day of the week: run errands for Mummy, dodge her slipper and the broom, reach her before she counts to three, greet visiting aunties and only take their money when she looks away. Full Home Wahala guide.

The Weekly Cup (real prizes)

Tap the Weekly Cup card on the menu. Your best single run counts, everyone gets the same game, and shop upgrades are off. When the cup ends the top players win real prizes and claim them on WhatsApp with a private code. There is an Office Cup (Monday to Friday) and a Home Cup (every day). See the winners and the rules.

Install on Android or iPhone

On Android, tap Install app on the menu. On iPhone, open the game in Safari and tap Install on iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. It then opens full screen and works without data.

Leagues for both games

When you create a private league you choose the game: Office Wahala (paused at weekends) or Home Wahala (open every day). League chips on the board show which one it is.

Ten tips to last longer

  1. Stay in the lower half of the screen: you get more time to react to things falling from the top.
  2. When Oga's warning appears, move away from that side before he arrives, then keep circling.
  3. Save Fake call for Oga or HR, and Auto-reply for when the screen is crowded.
  4. During a blackout, stay near the middle and move in small steps.
  5. Report desks are worth a risk early on; later, only stand on one when the area around it is clear.
  6. Leave approval makes you untouchable: use those five seconds to grab everything nearby.
  7. Close calls add up. Brushing past obstacles is free points if you're confident.
  8. Buy the ergonomic chair first; speed helps with everything.
  9. On the commute, the BRT lane is usually quieter, but watch the okadas.
  10. Play the daily shift each day: it's the fairest way to compare with colleagues.
