About Office Wahala

Office Wahala is a free arcade game about surviving a Nigerian workday, made in Lagos for anyone who has ever been asked to "do the needful".

Office Wahala is a free browser game built by Mmadubuko Micheal, CEO of Analaize Limited in Lagos, Nigeria. Players dodge their boss, meetings, deadlines and NEPA blackouts while collecting salary. It runs on phones and computers with no download or sign-up, works offline, and supports English and Nigerian Pidgin. It was first released in October 2026.

Why it exists

Every Nigerian office has the same characters: the Oga who appears exactly when you sit down, the colleague with "one small task", HR and their queries, and Aunty from Accounts guarding the salary. Office Wahala turns that shared experience into a quick game you can play on a break, laugh about with colleagues, and challenge each other on.

What's in the game

  • A Lagos morning commute, a full office day from 8:00am into overtime, and NEPA blackouts
  • Four bosses (Oga, the MD's driver, the HR lady and Aunty from Accounts), five shift events and three keep-for-later power-ups
  • Career mode with a promotion letter, a daily shift, challenge links and private office leagues
  • A staff shop, 8 outfits, 19 badges and two characters, Emeka and Adaeze
  • English and Pidgin, an original Afrobeats-style soundtrack and voice lines
  • Works offline and can be installed to a phone's home screen

How it's built

The game is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with an HTML canvas. All characters, artwork, music and sound effects are original and generated in code. There are no pop-up or video ads and no advertising trackers; the only ads are sponsor billboards drawn into the game, managed by the developer. Office leagues and anonymous gameplay statistics run on Netlify. The source is released under the MIT licence.
