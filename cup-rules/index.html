Weekly Cup rules

Free to enter. The highest score wins. Checked by a person before anyone is paid.

In short: play the Weekly Cup from the game menu during the cup dates. Your best single run counts. When the cup ends, winners claim on WhatsApp with a private code, and prizes are paid by bank transfer, airtime, gift card or voucher. See the winners page.

1. Who runs the cups

The Weekly Cups are run by Analaize Limited, Lagos, the developer of Office Wahala (contact: 07066459689 on WhatsApp).

Weekly leaderboards

The weekly leaderboards for Office Wahala and Home Wahala include every normal shift played that week (Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, Lagos time); no joining is needed. When the organiser offers prizes or credits for a week's leaderboard, these same rules apply to them, including the checks before payment and the claim code.

2. Entry is free

Anyone can take part without paying anything or buying anything. There are no sign-ups. The in-game naira wallet has nothing to do with the cups and can never be used to enter or to win.

3. How to win

Play the cup from the Weekly Cup card in the game during the dates shown. Only your best single run counts. Every player gets the same game for that cup and shop upgrades are switched off, so it is a test of skill. The highest scores when the cup ends win the prizes shown on the cup. If two scores are equal, the one reached first wins. Some cups limit the number of tries or the length of each run; the cup card says so.

4. Office Cup and Home Cup

Each cup is for one game, and the cup card shows which. An Office Cup is played in Office Wahala and is closed on Saturdays and Sundays (Lagos time). A Home Cup is played in Home Wahala and is open every day. There can be an Office Cup and a Home Cup in the same week, each with its own prizes and winners.

5. Checking scores

Before prizes are paid, the organiser checks the top scores. Scores that look impossible, that come from tampering with the game, from several devices or names used by the same person, or that break these rules can be disqualified, and the next player moves up. The organiser's decision is final.

6. Claiming a prize

When a cup ends, winners see a private claim code in the game on the device they played on. Tap Claim on WhatsApp to send the code to the organiser, together with how you would like to be paid. Prizes must be claimed within 30 days. A claim code only works for the player it was made for.

7. How prizes are paid

Prizes are paid by bank transfer, airtime, data, gift card, voucher or promo code, as shown on the cup. The organiser aims to pay within 3 working days of a valid claim and marks the prize as Paid on the winners page. Cash prizes cannot be swapped for something else unless the organiser offers it.

8. Age

Cash prizes are paid to players aged 18 or over. Younger winners receive airtime, a gift card or a voucher of the same value, or a parent or guardian can claim for them.

9. One prize per person

Each person can win at most one prize per cup. Using several devices or names to win more than once leads to disqualification.

10. Your data

The cups use the same random device ID as the rest of the game. The public board shows only the name you typed and, if you chose one, your state. Payment details are shared only with the organiser on WhatsApp by winners, used only to pay the prize and not stored in the game.

11. Changes

The organiser can change a cup's name, dates, prizes, tries or run length while it is running, for example to add a sponsor's prize. Scores already made are kept. The game a cup is played in does not change once people have played it.

12. Pausing and ending early

If something goes wrong (for example a bug, a network problem or abuse), the organiser can pause a cup. While a cup is paused, the board stays visible but nobody can start a new run; a run already started still counts. When the cup is resumed, play continues until the end time shown. The organiser can also end a cup early: the board at that moment is the final result, and winners claim in the usual way. A cup can also be cancelled, in which case it is removed and no prizes are given. Any change shows in the game and on the winners page.

13. Trophy badges

Players who have won a prize in a finished cup or weekly leaderboard get a small trophy next to their name on the boards and the winners page. A gold trophy with a number shows how many times they finished first. Trophies are for fun and do not affect scores.
