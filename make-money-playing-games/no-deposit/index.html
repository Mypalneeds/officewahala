Games that pay without a deposit or investment

The safest rule in online money: if you never send money, you cannot lose money. Here is how to keep it that way.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Games that pay without a deposit are free-entry contests where a sponsor or the game maker funds the prizes, such as the Office Wahala Weekly Cup. Be careful with platforms that start free and then ask for a small deposit to "upgrade", "unlock withdrawal" or "join VIP": that is how most losses begin.

Key facts

  • Office Wahala has no deposits, no wallet top-ups and no withdrawals.
  • The in-game naira cannot be bought or cashed out.
  • Prizes are funded by the developer or a named sponsor.
  • Nigeria's SEC advises checking that any platform taking money is registered.

How "no deposit" platforms get a deposit later

  • The upgrade: "Free users earn ₦50 a task; VIP users earn ₦2,000. VIP is only ₦5,000."
  • The unlock: your balance is ₦48,000 but you must pay ₦3,000 to withdraw.
  • The team bonus: you earn more for every friend who deposits, which turns you into the recruiter.
  • The tax: "CBN tax" or "clearance fee" before payout. Real taxes are not collected like this.

Why free contests can afford prizes

A free game earns from sponsors and brand partners, not from players. A cup with a few prizes a week costs the organiser a known, fixed amount. A platform that promises everyone returns has no fixed cost, which is why those platforms run out of money.

Questions people ask

Is there any game that pays without investment?

Yes: free-entry tournaments where prizes are funded by the organiser or sponsors. Only the winners get paid, and you never pay anything.

What if a game asks for a small fee to withdraw?

Do not pay it. A real prize is sent without fees, and paying once usually leads to more fees.

Can I lose money playing Office Wahala?

No. There is nothing to buy and nowhere to send money.
