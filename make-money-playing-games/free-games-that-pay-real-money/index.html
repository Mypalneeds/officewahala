Lots of games call themselves free and paying. Very few are both. Use this quick test before you spend your time and data.

The 30-second test

  1. Do I have to pay anything, ever? A deposit, an "activation", a VIP level, a withdrawal fee. If yes, it is not free.
  2. Who pays the prizes? A sponsor or the game maker is fine. New players' deposits are not.
  3. How are winners chosen? There should be a clear rule, like "highest score when the cup ends".
  4. Can I see past winners? Look for names, dates and whether they were paid.
  5. Can I contact a real person? A real phone number or business beats an anonymous Telegram admin.

Why "everyone earns" is a warning sign

Money has to come from somewhere. When an app promises that every player earns, the money usually comes from adverts (which pay very little per player) or from new users' deposits (which is how Ponzi schemes work). In a contest, a few people win and everyone else had fun for free. That is honest.
