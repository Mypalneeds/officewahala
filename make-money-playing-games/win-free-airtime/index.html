How to win free airtime playing games in Nigeria

Airtime is the most common prize in Nigerian online contests because it can be sent to any network in seconds. Here is how to win it honestly.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: You can win free airtime by playing free-entry game tournaments that list airtime as a prize, such as the Office Wahala Weekly Cup. Play during the cup, post a high score, and if you finish in the prize places you claim on WhatsApp and the airtime is sent to the number you choose. Ignore "free recharge" links that ask you to share them or enter your details first.

Key facts

  • Airtime prizes work on MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile.
  • Each Office Wahala cup lists its prizes for every place before you play.
  • You choose the number the airtime goes to when you claim.
  • Real contests never need your SIM PIN, OTP or BVN.

Why airtime is such a common prize

Airtime is small, fast and useful to everyone. An organiser can send it the same day without needing your bank details, which is why contests often use it for the places below 1st. Each Office Wahala cup shows its exact prizes on the cup card before you play.

The "free airtime" links to avoid

Messages such as "MTN is giving everyone ₦10,000 free recharge, share to 15 groups" are fake. Networks do not run giveaways through forwarded links. These pages collect phone numbers, push you to install apps, or ask for an OTP that lets someone take over your WhatsApp. If a link wants you to share it before you get anything, close it.

Winning airtime in Office Wahala

Open the game and look for the Weekly Cup card on the menu. It shows the prizes for each place. Your best single run counts. Every normal shift you play also counts on that week's top players board, and the organiser can credit top players with airtime too. Winners see a claim code in the game.

How to win and claim airtime

  1. Open Office Wahala. Go to officewahala.netlify.app on any phone. There is nothing to download.
  2. Tap the Weekly Cup card. Check the prizes and when the cup ends. Type the name you want on the board.
  3. Play your best run. Only your best single score counts, so you can keep trying (unless the cup limits tries).
  4. Check the board when the cup ends. If you are in the prize places, a claim code appears in the game.
  5. Claim on WhatsApp. Tap Claim on WhatsApp and send the code with the phone number for the airtime.

Questions people ask

Which networks can receive airtime prizes?

MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile. You choose the number when you claim.

How fast is airtime sent after I win?

The organiser aims to pay within 3 working days of a valid claim, and airtime is usually the fastest prize to send.

Can I win airtime without playing the cup?

Yes. Every normal Office Wahala or Home Wahala shift counts on the weekly top players board, and top players can be credited with airtime.

Is "share this link for free airtime" real?

No. Networks do not give airtime through forwarded links. Never enter an OTP or PIN on such pages.
