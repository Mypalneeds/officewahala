Is Office Wahala legit?

Good question to ask about any game that offers money. Here is everything you need to check us yourself.

Short answer: Yes. Office Wahala is a free browser game made by Analaize Limited, a Lagos company led by Mmadubuko Micheal. What Office Wahala is

A free arcade game about surviving a Nigerian workday: the Lagos commute, Oga, HR queries, the 5pm meeting and NEPA blackouts. At home, Home Wahala has Mummy's slipper and the visiting aunties. It plays in any phone browser in English or Pidgin. More about the game and the developer.

How prizes are funded

The developer funds cup prizes as promotion for the game, and brands can sponsor cups to reach players. Sponsors are named on the cup card, the share image and the winners page. Players never fund prizes.

How to check us yourself

  • Look at the winners page: every finished cup, its winners and whether each prize was paid.
  • Read the cup rules: who can win, how scores are checked, claim and payment times.
  • Message the developer on WhatsApp: 07066459689.
  • Notice what we never ask for: money, BVN, PIN, OTP or passwords.

Questions people ask

Does Office Wahala ask for money?

Never. There are no deposits, purchases or fees of any kind.

Can I sell my in-game naira?

No. It is pretend money for the shop and outfits, and it cannot be bought or cashed out.

What if I win and am not paid?

Message the developer on WhatsApp with your claim code. The organiser aims to pay within 3 working days of a valid claim and marks each prize Paid publicly.

Is there an Office Wahala app on the Play Store?

No. The official game is at officewahala.netlify.app. Treat any app using the name as unofficial.
