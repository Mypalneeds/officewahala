Skill games vs betting in Nigeria: what's the difference?

Both can pay money. They are not the same thing, and the difference matters for your pocket and the law.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: The key differences are stake and chance. Betting and lotteries mean paying to enter for an outcome you mostly cannot control. A free skill contest costs nothing to enter and the best performance wins. Paid betting is regulated in Nigeria, mainly by the states since the Supreme Court ruling of 22 November 2024. Office Wahala's Weekly Cups are free and decided by score.

Key facts

  • On 22 November 2024 the Supreme Court nullified the National Lottery Act 2005 for the states.
  • States now regulate lotteries in their territory; the federal government still covers the FCT.
  • In Lagos, gaming is overseen by the Lagos State Lotteries and Gaming Authority.
  • Office Wahala cash prizes go to players aged 18 or over.

Who regulates gaming in Nigeria now

Until 2024 the National Lottery Regulatory Commission licensed lotteries across the country. On 22 November 2024 the Supreme Court held that the National Assembly could not legislate on lotteries for the states, only for the FCT. State laws, such as Lagos's, now govern lotteries and gaming in each state, and lawyers note that online gaming across state lines is still being worked out. If a platform takes stakes, it should be licensed where you live. This is general information, not legal advice.

Age rules

Betting is for adults only. In Office Wahala, anyone can play, but cash prizes go only to players aged 18 or over; younger winners get airtime, a gift card or a voucher of the same value, or a parent or guardian can claim for them. See the cup rules.

Questions people ask

Is a free game with prizes gambling?

Generally, gambling involves paying a stake for a chance to win. A free contest decided by skill is different. Rules vary by state, so this is not legal advice.

Can students under 18 win Office Wahala prizes?

Yes, but not cash: they receive airtime, a gift card or a voucher, or a parent can claim for them.

Where can I get help with gambling problems?

If betting is hurting your finances or mood, talk to someone you trust, set blocks on betting sites with your bank, and seek professional support.
