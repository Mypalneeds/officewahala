Gaming and "earn money" app scams in Nigeria: 9 red flags

Most online money scams in Nigeria follow the same script. Learn the script and you will spot them early.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: The biggest red flags are: you must pay before you can earn, guaranteed daily returns, bigger rewards for recruiting than for playing, withdrawal fees or moving minimums, and requests for your BVN, PIN or OTP. The 9 red flags

  1. Pay to start. Registration fee, activation fee, starter pack, VIP level.
  2. Guaranteed returns. "Earn 5% daily", "double in 30 days".
  3. Recruit to earn. Team bonuses bigger than gameplay rewards.
  4. Withdrawal games. Fees, "tax", a minimum that rises, or "withdrawal day" only once a month.
  5. Your secrets. Requests for BVN, card PIN, OTP, internet banking password or a WhatsApp code.
  6. Urgency. "Only 50 slots left", "price goes up tonight".
  7. Fake proof. Screenshots of alerts and testimonials from accounts that only post about the platform.
  8. No real company. No address, no registration, admins only on Telegram.
  9. Moving rules. Terms change after you join, usually just before you can withdraw.

If you have already paid

  • Stop sending money, including "fees" to recover your funds; recovery offers are often a second scam.
  • Screenshot everything: chats, payment receipts, the website and the names used.
  • Call your bank to report the transfer and ask about blocking or recall.
  • Report investment schemes to the SEC, and fraud to the EFCC or the police.

Questions people ask

How do I check if an investment platform is registered?

Nigeria's SEC advises checking the company on its official registers before sending money. If you cannot find it, do not invest.

Are "task apps" that pay for likes and follows real?

Some pay tiny amounts at first to build trust, then ask for a deposit to unlock bigger tasks. Treat any deposit request as a stop sign.

Someone says I won a prize I never entered for. Is it real?

No. You cannot win a contest you did not enter. Do not click the link or share any codes.
