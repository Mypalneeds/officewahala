Win gift cards and supermarket vouchers playing games

When a brand sponsors a contest, the prize is often its own voucher. That is good news for players: sponsors fund more prizes.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Gift cards and vouchers usually come from sponsored contests: a shop, restaurant or app pays for the prizes in return for its name on the contest. Office Wahala Weekly Cups can be sponsored, and sponsored cups may list supermarket vouchers, gift cards or promo codes as prizes. You redeem them with the brand, following the terms on the voucher.

Key facts

  • Sponsor names show on the cup card, the share image and the winners page.
  • Vouchers have their own expiry dates and terms set by the brand.
  • A real gift card prize is sent to you; you never pay to "unlock" it.
  • Under-18 winners of cash cups receive vouchers or airtime instead.

Why sponsors give vouchers

A voucher brings a winner into the sponsor's shop or app, which is worth more to the brand than the same amount in cash. That is why sponsored cups can offer more prizes, or more places, than unsponsored ones.

Gift card scams to watch for

  • Someone "pays" you with a gift card code and asks for change in cash.
  • A message says you won a gift card but must buy another gift card to claim it.
  • A site asks for your card details to "verify" a free gift card.

A real voucher prize only needs you to say where to send it.

Questions people ask

Can I swap a voucher for cash?

Only if the organiser offers it. The cup rules say prizes are paid as shown on the cup.

Which shops are the vouchers for?

It depends on the sponsor of each cup. The cup card in the game shows the prizes before you play.

Do vouchers expire?

Yes, most do. Check the date and terms on the voucher and use it early.
