Online game tournaments in Nigeria with cash prizes

A good tournament is simple: clear rules, a fair contest and winners who get paid. Here is how to read any tournament before you play.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Online tournaments in Nigeria range from big esports events to free weekly score contests. In a score contest like the Office Wahala Weekly Cup, everyone plays the same game during the cup dates, your best single run counts, ties go to whoever reached the score first, and the organiser checks the top scores for cheating before paying winners.

Key facts

  • Office Wahala cups give every player the same game with shop upgrades switched off.
  • Some cups limit tries or run length; the cup card says so.
  • Suspicious scores are checked; cheaters are removed and the next player moves up.
  • The organiser can pause a cup if something goes wrong; the board stays visible.

Common tournament formats

  • Best score: play as often as you like; your highest single run counts (Office Wahala Weekly Cups).
  • Limited tries: the same, but with, say, 5 attempts.
  • Brackets: head-to-head matches, common in esports.
  • Leaderboard season: points add up over weeks.

What makes a tournament fair

Everyone should play the same version of the game, with no paid advantages. In Office Wahala cups, the office layout is the same for everyone and shop upgrades are off, so it comes down to skill. Equal scores are split by time: the player who reached the score first ranks higher.

How winners are checked

Before paying, the organiser looks at the top scores for signs of tampering: scores that rise too fast for the time played, levels that do not match the time survived, and many names from one person. Those are removed and the next player moves up. The decision is explained in the cup rules.

Questions people ask

How long does a Weekly Cup last?

Most run Monday to Sunday (Lagos time), but each cup shows its own start and end time.

What happens if a cup is paused?

The board stays visible but no new runs can start until it resumes. A run already started still counts.

Can a cup end early?

Yes, if something goes wrong. The board at that moment becomes the final result and winners claim as usual.

Is there a team or league option?

Yes. Office Wahala also has free private leagues for your office, school or friends, without prizes.
