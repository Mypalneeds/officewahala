How to make money playing games on your phone in Nigeria

You do not need a gaming PC. Most Nigerian players game on a phone, and so do most winners.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: On a phone, the realistic options are free browser tournaments (like Office Wahala's Weekly Cup, which needs no download), mobile esports in popular competitive games, and short game videos on social media. Start with free contests because they cost nothing; esports and content take much longer to pay.

Key facts

  • Office Wahala runs in the browser: no Play Store download, about the size of a few photos.
  • It works on older Android phones and iPhones.
  • Installing it to your home screen makes it open full screen and work offline.
  • Touch controls: drag to move; on a keyboard use the arrow keys.

Tips for playing to win on a phone

  • Turn on Do Not Disturb so a call does not end your best run.
  • Play with the phone on a table if your hands get tired; steady thumbs beat fast thumbs.
  • Close other apps on older phones so the game runs smoothly.
  • Charge before a serious attempt: low-power mode can slow games down.

Set up Office Wahala on your phone

  1. Open the site. Go to officewahala.netlify.app in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  2. Install it. Tap Install app on Android, or Share → Add to Home Screen → Add on iPhone.
  3. Set your name and state. Your name shows on the boards; your state counts for State Wars.
  4. Play the Weekly Cup. Tap the cup card on the menu and post your best run before it ends.

Questions people ask

Do I need to download an app?

No. Office Wahala is a website that you can add to your home screen. It is not on the Play Store, so beware of fake "Office Wahala" apps.

Will it work on my old Android phone?

It is designed to. If it is slow, close other apps and turn off battery saver while you play.

Can I play on iPhone?

Yes, in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen for the full-screen version.
