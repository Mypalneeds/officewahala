Games that pay directly to your bank account in Nigeria

A bank transfer is the prize everyone wants. It is also the prize scammers use as bait. Here is how to tell them apart.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Some free contests pay winners straight to a Nigerian bank account. In the Office Wahala Weekly Cup, 1st place prizes are often bank transfers. To receive one you only share your account name, account number and bank. Never share your BVN, card details, PIN, password or OTP: nobody needs those to send you money.

Key facts

  • To receive a transfer you need only account name, number and bank.
  • Your BVN, PIN, card number and OTP are never needed to pay you.
  • Office Wahala winners share bank details only on WhatsApp, only after winning.
  • Paid prizes are marked "Paid" on the public winners page.

If someone says you must pay a "transfer fee", "tax" or "activation fee" before your prize can be released, it is a scam. A real organiser simply sends the money.

How Office Wahala pays bank transfer prizes

When a cup ends, winners see a private claim code in the game. They tap Claim on WhatsApp, send the code and their account details, and the organiser checks the score before paying. The organiser aims to pay within 3 working days of a valid claim and then marks the prize as Paid on the winners page. Cash prizes go to players aged 18 or over; younger winners get airtime, a gift card or a voucher, or a parent can claim.

Questions people ask

Do I need a bank account to win?

No. If you do not want to share bank details, you can ask for a different prize type when the cup offers it, such as airtime or a voucher.

Will I be charged a fee to receive my prize?

No. Office Wahala never charges winners anything. Any "fee" request is a scam.

How do I know Office Wahala really pays?

Every finished cup, its winners and their payment status are listed on the public winners page.

Is my bank number shown publicly?

No. The board shows only the name you typed and, if you chose one, your state. Payment details stay in your WhatsApp chat with the organiser.
