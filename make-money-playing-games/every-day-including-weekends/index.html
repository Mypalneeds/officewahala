Win prizes playing games every day, even at weekends

Office Wahala closes at weekends, because nobody should be at work on Saturday. Home Wahala does not.

By Mmadubuko Micheal, Analaize Limited, Lagos · Updated 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: In Office Wahala, the Office Cup runs Monday to Friday (Lagos time) and the office is closed on Saturday and Sunday. Home Wahala, a day at home dodging Mummy's slipper, is open every day, and its Home Cup and weekly Home leaderboard keep running through the weekend.

Key facts

  • Each cup is for one game: Office or Home.
  • Office shifts, career mode and office leagues pause at weekends.
  • Home shifts count on the Home weekly board every day.
  • Weekly boards reset every Monday at midnight, Lagos time.

What is Home Wahala?

Home Wahala is the second game in Office Wahala: a Nigerian childhood day at home. Dodge the slipper, the broom and "I will count to three", run errands, greet the visiting aunties and do not touch the meat in the pot. Read the Home Wahala guide.

A simple weekly plan

DayPlay
Monday to FridayOffice Cup and/or Home Cup; every normal shift counts on its weekly board
Saturday and SundayHome Cup and the Home weekly board
Sunday nightLast chance before the weekly boards reset at midnight

Play the Home Cup at the weekend

  1. Open Office Wahala. On Saturday or Sunday the menu switches you to Home Wahala automatically.
  2. Tap the Weekly Cup card. At weekends it shows the Home Cup, with its prizes and end time.
  3. Learn Mummy's patterns. Watch for the slipper wind-up and the count to three, and greet every aunty.
  4. Post your best run. Only your best single score counts, so keep trying until the cup ends.

Questions people ask

Why is Office Wahala closed at weekends?

Because it is a game about the Nigerian workday, and nobody should be at work on Saturday. The shop, your desk and leaderboards stay open.

Which time zone do cups use?

Lagos time (West Africa Time, UTC+1).

Can I play both cups in the same week?

Yes. They are separate cups with separate prizes and winners.
